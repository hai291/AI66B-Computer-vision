Issue #5 - DenseNet cho crop MRI

Chon DenseNet121: khoang 7,98 trieu tham so voi dau ImageNet 1000 lop,
it hon DenseNet169 (14,15 trieu). Dau ra trong bai nay duoc doi thanh 3 lop.
Ban 1 kenh, 3 lop trong model_densenet.py co 6.950.659 tham so.
DenseNet noi dac trung cua cac lop truoc vao lop sau, giup tai su dung dac trung.
Day la lua chon khoi dau de thu nghiem, chua phai bien the tot nhat da duoc chung minh.
Nguon: https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/main/models/generated/torchvision.models.densenet121.html
       https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/main/models/generated/torchvision.models.densenet169.html

Crop la cat mot o nho quanh diem (x, y) duoc danh dau tren anh MRI.
Vi du tam (200, 250), canh 128: lay cot 136:264 va hang 186:314.
Trong mang anh, thu tu la anh[y, x]. Resize chi doi kich thuoc, khong chon vung.
Code crop 128x128 pixel goc, them vien den neu cham mep, resize thanh 224x224.
Moi crop lay nhan tu train.csv theo study_id + condition + level.
Bo qua nhan thieu; thieu file anh thi dung va bao duong dan, khong tu bo qua.
Tat ca condition duoc hoc chung trong mot model phan loai 3 muc do.

Anh xam duoc min-max tren tung lat roi chuan hoa ve [-1, 1] cho ca hai model.
DenseNet doi convolution dau thanh 1 kenh bang tong trong so 3 kenh RGB.
Day la dieu chinh cho MRI; khong dung nguyen bo chuan hoa RGB cua ImageNet.
Fine-tune toan bo DenseNet pretrained. Baseline LumbarCNN khoi tao ngau nhien.
Vi vay so sanh nay la hai cau hinh thuc te, khong tach rieng tac dong cua pretraining.
Them --no-pretrained neu muon so sanh ca hai model huan luyen tu dau.

Chay tren Kaggle

1. Dang nhap, vao cuoc thi va chap nhan dieu khoan truy cap dataset:
   https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-2024-lumbar-spine-degenerative-classification
2. Tao Notebook, import file densenet_kaggle.ipynb trong repo.
3. Trong Add Input/Add Data, gan dataset cua cuoc thi vao notebook.
   Can train.csv, train_label_coordinates.csv va train_images.
   Khong can tai toan bo anh ve may; dataset duoc doc tai /kaggle/input.
4. Trong Settings chon Accelerator la GPU duoc cap, bat Internet de clone repo
   va tai pretrained weights. Script chi dung mot GPU, ke ca duoc cap T4 x2.
5. Chay tung cell. Cell xem crop de kiem tra vi tri. Cell smoke chi dung 20 ca,
   1 epoch de kiem tra code; khong dung ket qua nay lam so sanh cuoi.
6. Chay hai cell train day du. Dau ra nam trong /kaggle/working/runs.
   Neu het bo nho GPU, doi batch-size thanh 8 cho ca hai lenh.
   Khi xong, Save Version va luu outputs; tai runs ve de giu checkpoint/metric.
   Moi lan thu cau hinh moi can ten output moi, tranh ghi de ket qua cu.

Lenh tren may ca nhan (chay tu thu muc repo)

python -m pip install -r requirements-densenet.txt
python train_densenet.py --data-root data --model baseline --output runs/baseline
python train_densenet.py --data-root data --model densenet --output runs/densenet
python train_densenet.py --data-root data --evaluate runs/densenet

Cau hinh mac dinh: train/val 80/20 theo study_id, seed 42, 5 epoch,
batch 16, Adam lr=0.001, CrossEntropy khong trong so, khong augmentation.
CNN baseline va DenseNet dung cung danh sach crop, preprocessing, split va metric.
train_demo.py cu chi co anh gia va train accuracy, khong co baseline MRI validation.
Vi vay can chay lai LumbarCNN tren du lieu that bang lenh tren de so sanh.
Chon checkpoint co validation log loss thap nhat trong 5 epoch cho ca hai model.
Day la holdout validation; chua co ket qua tren tap test doc lap.

Trong moi thu muc output:
config.json: cau hinh, so tham so, device va phien ban torch.
samples.csv: danh sach crop, nhan va split train/val de doi chieu.
history.csv: train loss va validation metric tung epoch.
best.pt: checkpoint co validation log loss thap nhat.
metrics.json: log loss, accuracy, macro-F1, confusion matrix cua checkpoint tot nhat.
predictions.csv: xac suat tung crop validation, co condition va level de phan tich tiep.
Confusion matrix: hang la nhan that, cot la du doan; thu tu Normal/Mild, Moderate, Severe.
Cell cuoi notebook tao comparison.csv tu ket qua hai model va kiem tra cung split.

Ket qua hien tai: chua train MRI that, chua co metric de ket luan hon baseline.
Can cap nhat comparison.csv va nhan xet sau khi chay Kaggle; khong dien so uoc luong.
Metric o day tinh theo crop co toa do cho san, khong phai metric RSNA day du.
Khong dung toa do validation de train. Tuy nhien, validation van can toa do that
de cat anh, nen chi danh gia buoc phan loai khi da biet vi tri, chua tu dinh vi.
Nhieu toa do cua cung condition-level tao nhieu crop, moi crop tinh mot mau.

Uu diem du kien: tap trung vao vung can hoc, tai su dung dac trung va pretrained weights.
Han che: can toa do, crop co the mat ngu canh; 128 pixel khong cung kich thuoc vat ly
giua cac may chup. DenseNet ton bo nho activation va cham hon CNN nho.
Accuracy co the cao chi vi lop Normal/Mild dong; can xem macro-F1 va confusion matrix.
Toi uu tiep sau khi co ket qua: thu crop-size va learning rate tung cai mot,
giu nguyen split, ghi lai cau hinh, khong chon ket qua dua tren test.
